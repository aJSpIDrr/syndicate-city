# Syndicate City — Art List

**Already in the game:** rank portraits + signature weapon tiers for The Five Families, The Firm, The Triad and The Syndicate. Page backgrounds, header banners and faction cards for **all 6 factions**. District scenes for **all 8 districts**.

Everything below has a slot ready in the game. Any slot without art uses the built-in drawings, so upload in any order.

## How to deliver
- **Make sheets (grids), like your faction sheets.** Uniform cells, thin dark gutters between cells, cells filled **left to right, top to bottom** in the order listed. I cut them up automatically.
- **One sheet per file, JPG, under 10 MB** (about 2000–2500 px wide is plenty).
- **Exception:** page backgrounds and header banners are big single images, so send those as separate files.
- **Style:** the same bold comic-noir as your faction sheets, with dark backgrounds and the subject centred.
- **Weapons:** if a sheet shows a different weapon than listed, the game gets renamed to match the art.
